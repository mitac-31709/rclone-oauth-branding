Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-10-13

Who this applies to

This OAuth client is for personal, single-user use by the project owner only. It is not offered as a public product or multi-tenant service.

What data is accessed

With the user’s consent, the local rclone process may read and write files in that user’s Google Drive solely to store and manage an encrypted restic backup repository.

What we collect / store

  • No website analytics or accounts on these GitHub Pages.
  • No server-side storage of Drive contents or OAuth tokens. Tokens are kept in the user’s local rclone config.
  • Backup payload is encrypted by restic before upload; the OAuth client does not receive the restic encryption password.

Third parties

Authentication is handled by Google. Usage of Google APIs is subject to Google’s policies. These static pages are hosted on GitHub Pages.

Contact

Questions about this personal client: GitHub issues.
